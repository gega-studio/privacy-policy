JuiceLoop Privacy Policy

App: JuiceLoop — Recipes & Journal (com.gegastudio.juiceloop)
Developer: Gega Studio
Version 1.0 · Last updated: 28 August 2026

The short version: everything stays on your device. JuiceLoop works entirely offline. It has no account, collects no personal information, uses no analytics and contains no advertising software.

What we collect

Nothing. Gega Studio does not collect, receive, transmit or store any personal information from JuiceLoop. There is no server component.

What the app stores on your device

JuiceLoop saves the following inside its own private app storage, where no other app can read it:

  • The recipe book that ships with the app.
  • Your reviews (taste, freshness, fullness, whether you would make it again, actual yield, notes and substitutions).
  • Your preparation history and favourites.
  • Your challenges, plan and calendar entries.
  • Your pantry items, including purchase and expiry dates you enter.
  • Your shopping lists.
  • Your settings: juicer type, glass size, measurement system, challenge repetition, dietary preferences and ingredients to avoid.

What the app sends

Nothing. JuiceLoop declares no internet permission in its manifest, so it is not technically capable of sending data anywhere, even accidentally.

Permissions

JuiceLoop requests no permissions that give it access to you or your device. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, calendar or advertising identifier, and it has no internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.

For completeness: the build contains one permission that the app defines for itself, named com.gegastudio.juiceloop.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. It is added automatically by Google's AndroidX libraries, is marked "signature" level, and exists only so the app's own components can talk to each other. It grants no access to anything on your device and is not shown in the Android permissions screen.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

There are none. The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising SDK and no tracking of any kind. It does not use the Android Advertising ID.

Sharing a shopping list

If you tap Share on a shopping list, Android passes the list as plain text to whichever app you choose. That is the only situation in which information leaves JuiceLoop, it happens only when you ask for it, and what happens to the text afterwards is governed by the app you sent it to.

Backups

If you have Android's backup feature switched on, your device may include JuiceLoop's database and settings in your own Google account backup. That backup belongs to you and is controlled by your Android settings; Gega Studio has no access to it.

Children

JuiceLoop is a general-audience cooking and journalling app. It collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling JuiceLoop removes everything it has stored. You can also clear the app's data from Android Settings → Apps → JuiceLoop → Storage at any time. There is nothing held elsewhere to request or delete.

Health disclaimer

JuiceLoop is a cooking and journalling app, not medical advice. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Nutrition figures shown in the app are rough estimates that vary with produce size, ripeness and juicer efficiency. Speak to a doctor, pharmacist or dietitian before making juice a regular habit if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have diabetes or kidney disease, take prescription medication, or are preparing drinks for a young child. Grapefruit in particular interacts with a number of prescription medicines.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of JuiceLoop ever collects anything, this policy will be updated and the change will be described in the app's release notes before that version ships.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Gega Studio using the support contact published on the app's Google Play listing.
