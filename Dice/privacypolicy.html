Privacy Policy — Dice Roll Premium

Effective date: 4 September 2026  •  Last updated: 4 September 2026

The short version

  • All app data stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • No personal data is collected.
  • No internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or broad-storage permissions.

1. Who we are

Dice Roll Premium (“the app”) is developed and published by Gega Studio (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the app handles information. It applies to the Dice Roll Premium Android application.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no account system, no login, and no server. It does not gather your name, email, contacts, location, device identifiers, or usage analytics, and it does not transmit data off your device.

3. Data the app stores locally

To work as a dice roller, the app saves the following only on your device, in the app’s private storage:

  • Settings & preferences — theme, sound, haptics, and display options.
  • Custom dice — dice you create (numeric ranges or text faces).
  • Presets — saved roll configurations.
  • Roll history — records of your past rolls (kept up to a limit you choose).
  • Statistics — on-device tallies derived from your rolls.

This data never leaves your device unless you deliberately export it (see below). Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes it.

4. Export & import

You can optionally export a backup (JSON) of your presets, custom dice, and settings, or export your history (CSV). When you do, the app writes a file to a location you choose using Android’s system file picker. That file is entirely under your control. Importing only reads a file you explicitly select; imported content is validated and never executed.

5. Third-party services

The app uses no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. It makes no network requests and includes no advertising identifiers.

6. Permissions

The app requests no runtime permissions and declares none of its own in its manifest. It has no Internet permission and works fully offline.

Technical note: modern Android apps include a single app-private, signature-level permission (…DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION) that the AndroidX libraries add to secure their own internal components. It is not user-facing and grants no access to your data, network, or device sensors.

7. Device backups

If you enable Android’s system backup, your device’s operating system may include the app’s local data in its own OS-managed, encrypted backup. This is handled by Android — not by us — and can be turned off in your device settings.

8. Children

The app contains no ads, no purchases, and no data collection, making it suitable for a general audience, including children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

9. Security

Because your data is stored locally and never transmitted, it is protected by your device’s own security (lock screen, storage encryption). We hold no copy of your data on any server.

10. Data retention & your rights

You are in full control of your data. You can clear roll history, reset statistics, and delete presets and custom dice within the app at any time, or remove everything by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for you to request from us and no account to delete.

11. Randomness disclaimer

The app is intended for games and general entertainment only. It does not provide certified or “gambling-grade” randomness and makes no guarantee of statistical fairness.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the version distributed with the app and revise the date at the top of this page.
