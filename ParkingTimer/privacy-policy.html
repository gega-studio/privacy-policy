What the app does

Parking Timer helps you remember when your parking session expires. You choose a duration or an exact expiry time, start a countdown, optionally add parking details, and receive reminders before your parking runs out. It works fully offline and requires no sign-in.

Information stored on your device

All of the following is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, and is never sent to Gega Studio or any third party:

  • Parking sessions and history — start time, expiry time, extensions, and completion status.
  • Optional parking details you enter — vehicle or plate, zone, level, spot, cost, and notes.
  • App settings — reminder preferences, theme, time format, and defaults.

Notifications

On Android 13 and later, the app asks permission to post notifications so it can remind you before and at parking expiry. This is optional — the timer works if you decline, and you can change it anytime in system settings.

Exact alarms

The app uses Android's exact-alarm scheduling so reminders fire at the intended time. On newer Android versions you may be asked to allow this in system settings. If exact alarms are unavailable, the app falls back to inexact scheduling and tells you reminders may be slightly delayed. Reminder delivery can also be affected by your device's battery-optimization settings.

Optional location

Only if you tap "Save parking location," the app captures your current location once so you can find your car later. It is stored locally with the parking session and is never transmitted. The app requests foreground location only, never background location, and does not track you continuously. You can remove a saved location at any time. "Find my car" simply opens the saved coordinates in whatever map app you already have installed.

Optional parking photo

Only if you attach a parking photo, the app copies the image you pick into its own private storage so it stays available. It uses the Android photo picker and does not request broad access to your photo library. The photo is never transmitted and can be removed at any time.

Because the optional location and photo features are processed on your device only and never transmitted, Gega Studio does not receive this information. We do not claim the app processes nothing — rather, anything it processes stays on your device.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking, or telemetry.
  • No cloud storage, servers, or synchronization.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • The app does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send data off the device.

Backups

If you enable Android system backup, Android may include the app's local data (history, settings) in your own device backup or device-to-device transfer — controlled entirely by you and your Google account. Temporary photo captures are excluded. Gega Studio has no access to these backups.

How to erase your data

  • End or delete individual parking sessions in the app.
  • Open Settings → Clear parking history to remove all completed sessions.
  • Open Settings → Clear all local app data to erase every session (including any active one), stored photos, and reset settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its local data from your device.

Children

Parking Timer is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children.
