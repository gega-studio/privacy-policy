VYRENO

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026

Privacy at a glance

VYRENO is an offline-first learning app. We do not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or use your personal data for advertising or tracking. Your learning information stays on your device unless you explicitly export it and choose where to send it.

Information we collect

VYRENO does not send personal information, usage information, or learning activity to the developer or to a backend service. The app does not require an account.

The app stores the following information locally so its learning features work:

  • Onboarding goals, experience level, learning pace, appearance, and learning settings
  • Lesson and exercise completion, answers, reflections, and learning time
  • Quiz attempts and scores
  • Project status, checklist progress, and working notes
  • User-created notes, pinned items, favorites, and favorite prompts
  • Review schedules, learning sessions, streak information, and achievements

Storage, retention, and deletion

Learning data is stored in the app’s private on-device container using Apple’s SwiftData framework. It remains there until you delete it, reset your progress, or uninstall the app.

You can remove learning records with the reset control in VYRENO’s Settings screen. Reset requires confirmation. Uninstalling VYRENO removes its local app container in accordance with iOS behavior.

Export and import

Export is always user initiated. VYRENO creates a versioned JSON backup and opens Apple’s share interface. The file leaves the app only if you choose a destination. Any destination you select may apply its own privacy policy.

Import is also user initiated. VYRENO reads only the file you select, validates it, and asks for confirmation before replacing local learning data. Invalid or unsupported files are rejected.

Analytics, tracking, and third parties

VYRENO contains no advertising SDKs, third-party analytics, behavioral tracking, social trackers, or embedded AI-provider credentials. It does not use your information to track you across apps or websites.

Downloading and updating the app is handled by Apple and may be subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy. That processing is separate from VYRENO’s on-device learning features.

Device permissions

VYRENO does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, Bluetooth, health information, tracking identifier, or notifications.

Children’s privacy

VYRENO does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. Because no account or remote data collection is used, the app does not create a developer-held profile for any user.

Changes to this policy

If VYRENO’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before the affected functionality is released. The effective date at the top will identify the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact the developer using the Support URL shown on VYRENO’s App Store product page.
