Privacy Policy

Rest Timer Gym — by Gega Studio

App: Rest Timer GymDeveloper: Gega StudioLast updated: September 1, 2026

Rest Timer Gym is designed to be private by default. No account · No ads · No tracking

The app runs entirely on your device. It does not require an internet connection for its core features, it does not create an account, and it does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information. This policy explains exactly what that means.

Information we collect

None. Rest Timer Gym does not collect any personal or sensitive user data. Specifically, the app has:

  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No third-party tracking or marketing SDKs
  • No personal profile
  • No location collection
  • No access to contacts
  • No financial or payment information
  • No health measurements or fitness data collected about you

Because the app collects nothing, there is nothing for us to sell or share. Your personal information is never sold and never shared with third parties.

Data stored on your device

Everything you create in the app is stored locally on your device only and is never uploaded to us or anyone else:

  • Rest history — the rests you complete stay on your device.
  • Presets — your saved and custom timers stay on your device.
  • Settings — your preferences (sound, vibration, theme, and so on) stay on your device.

You can delete individual history entries, clear all history, or reset all app data at any time from within the app’s Settings screen. Uninstalling the app also removes this data.

Permissions and why they are used

The app requests only the minimum permissions needed for a timer to work reliably. None of them are used to collect information about you.

PermissionWhy it is used
NotificationsTo show the live countdown and the “rest complete” alert so the timer is useful when the app is in the background or your screen is off. Optional — the timer still runs if you decline.
VibrationTo provide a haptic alert when a rest finishes.
Foreground serviceTo keep the active countdown running accurately while the app is minimized or the phone is locked.
Wake lockTo keep the timer counting so it can alert you on time when the screen is off. Held only while a timer is actively running.

No internet access

The app does not request the Internet permission and does not make network requests for its core functionality. Your data does not leave your device through the app.

Android backup & restore

Android may include the app’s locally stored data (such as presets, history, and settings) in the operating system’s own backup and device-transfer features if you have those enabled for your device or Google account. This is handled entirely by Android, not by us, and any such backup is governed by Google’s terms and your device settings. You can disable Android backup for the app in your system settings.

Children’s privacy

Rest Timer Gym is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. Because the app does not collect any personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will be revised and the updated version will be published at the same location. Material changes will be reflected here.
