Pen Album — Privacy Policy

Developer: Gega Studio

Application: Pen Album (`com.gegastudio.penalbum`)

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Summary

Pen Album does not collect any personal information. It has no account system, no advertising, no

analytics and no Internet permission. Everything you record — the pens you own, your wishlist,

your notes and your photographs — stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. Gega Studio does not collect, receive, transmit, store, sell or share any information

about you or your use of Pen Album.

The app declares no Android permissions. It does not request access to your location, contacts,

camera, microphone, calendar, phone, device identifiers or accounts, and it does not have

permission to access the Internet.

Information the app stores on your device

Pen Album saves the following in its own private storage, where no other app can read it:

  • Your collection records — which pens you have marked as owned, how many copies of each, your

wishlist, your favourites, and the dates you recorded them.

  • Your notes — any text you type about a pen.
  • Your photographs — images you choose to attach to a pen.
  • Your settings — theme, colour, grid size, vibration and celebration preferences.

This information never leaves your device unless you deliberately export it (see below). It is

deleted when you uninstall the app, and you can erase it at any time from

Settings → Reset collection.

### Photographs

Photographs are chosen through the Android Photo Picker. The picker gives Pen Album access to

exactly the image you select and nothing else; the app never receives access to your photo library.

The selected image is copied into the app's private storage, resized and compressed. It is never

uploaded anywhere.

Files you export

Settings → Export collection lets you save a JSON backup or a CSV summary of your collection.

You choose where the file is written using the Android file picker. Once saved, the file is yours

and is outside the app's control; if you place it in a folder that syncs to a cloud service, that

service's own privacy policy applies to it. Exported files contain your collection records and

notes. They do not contain image data.

Backups made by your device

If you have enabled Android's built-in backup or device-transfer features, the operating system

may include Pen Album's collection database and settings in the backup or transfer that you

initiate. This is a function of the Android platform and of the account you configured with it,

not something Gega Studio operates or has access to. Photographs are excluded from cloud backup

and are only included in a direct device-to-device transfer.

Children

Pen Album is suitable for children. It contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no social

features, no messaging, no user-to-user contact and no external content feed. It does not collect

any personal information from anyone, including children.

Actions that leave the app or that permanently delete data — opening an email or a website,

exporting, importing and resetting the collection — are placed behind a simple multiplication

question intended for an adult. That question does not ask for and does not record a birth date,

an age, a name, an email address or any other personal information.

Third parties

Pen Album contains no third-party advertising network, analytics service, crash reporting service

or tracking library. No data is shared with any third party, because no data is collected.

Your rights and choices

Because no information is collected, there is nothing for Gega Studio to access, correct, export

or delete on your behalf. You remain in full control of the data stored on your own device:

  • Export it at any time from Settings → Export collection.
  • Delete all of it at any time from Settings → Reset collection.
  • Delete everything by uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Pen Album changes what it stores or introduces any feature that transmits

data, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the change will be

described in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Gega Studio at the support address published on the

store listing and on the app's About screen.
