Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

Split Bill does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no network access. The app works entirely on your device.

Data we store

The bills, people, items and preferences you create are stored only in the app's private storage on your device, using a local database. No one else can read them, including us.

Network

The app requests no internet permission and makes no network calls. It works identically in airplane mode.

Sharing

When you tap Share or export a receipt, Android hands the content you chose to the app you pick. Split Bill neither stores nor sees where it goes.

Deleting your data

Deleting a bill removes it and everything inside it immediately. Uninstalling the app removes all remaining data from your device.

Children

Because no data is collected, the app is safe for users of any age.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Gega Studio through the store listing for Split Bill.
