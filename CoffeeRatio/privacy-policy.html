Privacy Policy — Coffee Ratio Calculator

App: Coffee Ratio Calculator  •  Developer: Gega Studio
Effective date: 7 August 2026  •  Contact: your-contact-email

Coffee Ratio Calculator is a calculator that runs entirely on your device. It does not collect, store, share, sell or transmit any personal information, and it has no account, no advertising and no analytics. The only information it saves is your in-app preferences, and those stay on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect or process any personal or sensitive information. There is no sign-up, no profile, and no user data is sent anywhere.

Preferences stored on your device

To remember how you like the app set up, Coffee Ratio Calculator saves your preferences locally on your device only. These are settings such as:

  • theme (system, light or dark);
  • default calculation direction, units, ratio and brew method;
  • decimal precision and haptic-feedback preference;
  • whether the one-time introduction has been shown.

This information never leaves your device. It is not linked to your identity, is not transmitted to us or any third party, and is stored using the operating system's standard on-device app storage.

Removing this information: you can delete all saved preferences at any time by clearing the app's data (Settings → Apps → Coffee Ratio Calculator → Storage → Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app.

Network and offline operation

The app works fully offline and does not request the internet permission. It does not connect to any server or backend, so no data is uploaded, downloaded or synced.

Advertising and analytics

The app contains no advertisements, no analytics or measurement SDKs, and no tracking libraries. It does not use the Android Advertising ID.

Device features the app does not use

Coffee Ratio Calculator does not access your location, contacts, photos, media, files, camera or microphone. It does not process payments and does not create or use any account.

Data sharing and selling

We do not share or sell any data, because we do not collect any.

Permissions

The app declares no permissions of its own. The final app package includes a single signature-level permission (…DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION) that the Android libraries add automatically to protect the app's own internal components. It is scoped to this app, is never requested from you, and grants nothing to other apps or to us.

Children

The app is a general-purpose calculator suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
